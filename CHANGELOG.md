## 0.1.0

- Initial Dart port of Maslosoft Signals.
- Typed emission to constructor, method, and property receivers, with an
  independent signal clone for each receiver.
- Provider gathering, slot-aware emitted signals, reusable definitions, and
  pre/post filters.
- `SlotFor` and `SignalFor` annotations with a source generator and command-line
  build and preview commands.
- Package metadata, documentation, examples, and automated validation for pub.dev.
